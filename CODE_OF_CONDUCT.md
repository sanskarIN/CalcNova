# CalcNova Community Code of Conduct

CalcNova aims to provide a welcoming, constructive, and professional open-source community for contributors and users with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

Community participants should:

- discuss ideas and code respectfully;
- critique technical work rather than attacking people;
- assume good intent while still addressing concrete problems directly;
- make room for newcomers to ask questions;
- give actionable feedback;
- respect privacy and personal boundaries;
- avoid publishing another person's private information;
- keep issue and pull-request discussions relevant to the project;
- cooperate with maintainers when conversations need to be refocused.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable in CalcNova community spaces:

- harassment, intimidation, threats, or targeted abuse;
- discriminatory or demeaning remarks;
- sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention;
- deliberate personal attacks or repeated hostile behavior;
- doxxing or sharing private contact/location information without permission;
- impersonation or deliberate misrepresentation of another contributor;
- spam, scams, malicious links, or intentionally deceptive technical advice;
- publishing sensitive security details after being asked to use the private reporting process;
- sustained disruption of issues, reviews, or community discussions.

## Technical disagreement

Strong technical disagreement is allowed. Keep it evidence-based and specific. Useful disagreement explains tradeoffs, failure modes, compatibility risks, test evidence, or maintainability concerns without insulting the people involved.

## Maintainer response

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove community content that violates these expectations. Depending on severity and repetition, maintainers may warn a participant, limit participation, or block an account from project spaces.

When possible, moderation decisions should be proportionate to the behavior and focused on keeping the project safe and productive.

## Scope

This code applies to CalcNova-managed repositories and project community spaces, and to public interactions where someone is clearly representing the CalcNova project.

## Good-faith use

This document is intended to support healthy collaboration, not to suppress reasonable technical criticism or normal differences of opinion.
