## Release scope

CalcNova 1.0.0 is a complete local-first calculator. Included:

- standard, scientific and exact-rational arithmetic, engineering notation, and a programmer mode with bases 2–36 and fixed-width bitwise operations;
- Unicode code-point inspection, unit conversion, statistics including bivariate regression, equation solving, matrices and graphing;
- currency conversion with an offline-capable cache, calculation history with search, favourites and export, and persisted settings with schema migration;
- English and Hindi localization, keyboard-first operation, adaptive layout, high contrast and reduced motion;
- Desktop, Browser/WebAssembly, Android and iOS application heads sharing one view-model layer.
